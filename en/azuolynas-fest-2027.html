Festival Theme & Concept

Cinematic Celebration of Finding Your Calling

What do you want to do with your life, and how do you find your true calling? We are looking for stories about discovering your path, making career choices, and overcoming self-doubt. Tell us what it takes to follow your passion when the people around you expect something different—whether it is family pressure to pursue a "serious" career like medicine or law, or your own fear of stepping into the arts, music, or creative fields. Show the reality of this journey, the obstacles along the way, and the exact moment you or your characters realized what you were meant to do.

Regulations

Submission Rules & Criteria

  • 1 Smartphones/tablets only: The film must be shot strictly on a mobile phone or tablet. Professional cinema cameras are prohibited.
  • 2 Maximum runtime: Strictly up to 3 minutes (180 s) including credits.
  • 3 Submission deadline: All entries must be registered by March 26th via the festival portal.
  • 4 Student authorship: One entry per participant. The creative vision must belong genuinely to the youth creators themselves.
Age Categories

Competition Sections

CATEGORY I
Junior Category (10–13 years)

CATEGORY II
Senior Category (14–18 years)
Dedicated to high school students and young directors. Evaluates cinematic language, depth, composition, and bold vision.
