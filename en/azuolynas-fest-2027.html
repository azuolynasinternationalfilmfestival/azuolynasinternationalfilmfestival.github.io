About The Festival

Celebration of Youth Filmmaking

Get ready for the 7th International Youth Short Film Festival "Ąžuolynas FEST 2027"! For seven consecutive years, Kaunas International Gymnasium has stood at the crossroads of international student cinematic art.

We invite students aged 10 to 18 to create short films up to 3 minutes using smartphone cameras only. The official 2027 theme is "My Purpose" (Mano pašaukimas / My Fate). What inspires me? What is my life's calling, passion, and direction?

The gala festival ceremony takes place on April 23, 2027 at Kaunas International Gymnasium. Experience an international jury evaluation, handmade oak trophies, gala screening, and worldwide live broadcast!

Rules & Guidelines

Key Participant Requirements

Simple, transparent, and fair rules for all young creators worldwide.

📱

Phone or Tablet Only

All entries must be captured on a smartphone or tablet camera. Professional cinema equipment is strictly forbidden.

⏱️

Up to 3 Minutes (180s)

Strict runtime limit – up to 3 minutes including credits. Concise storytelling and creative framing are prized.

🌍

Two Age Categories

Category I: Students aged 10–13.
Category II: Students aged 14–18 from all countries.

🏆

Oak Trophies & Awards

Grand Prix, 1st place prizes, Audience Favorite diplomas, and custom artisan oak statuettes for laureates.
