History & Geography

Youth Cinema Center Across Four Continents

For the sixth consecutive year, our gymnasium has become a vibrant hub of youth cinematic art. The school proudly hosted the traditional international students film festival "Ąžuolynas". The scale of this year's festival is truly remarkable: as noted by Marija Daunorienė, Deputy Director for Education acting as Director, the geography of participants expanded to four continents. Young talents from Europe, Asia, North America, and Africa competed in the festival! Mastery and storytelling were evaluated by an esteemed international jury consisting of television and international relations experts from Italy, Republika Srpska, and Lithuania.
