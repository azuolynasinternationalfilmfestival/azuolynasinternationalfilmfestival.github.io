Guidelines

Terms of Participation

  • 1 Your film must be filmed strictly on a tablet or smartphone.
  • 2 The duration cannot exceed 3 minutes (180 s including credits).
  • 3 From one participant: only one film entry.
  • 4 Films made with adult assistance will not be considered.
  • 5 Your film must have English subtitles.
  • 6 The festival screening recording will be hosted directly on this website.
