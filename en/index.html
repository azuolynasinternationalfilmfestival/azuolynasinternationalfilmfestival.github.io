Submission Checklist

4 quick steps to a valid entry
  • 1
    Phone or Tablet: Any smartphone or tablet is accepted (iOS/Android). Mobile rigs and tripods are allowed.
  • 2
    Max 180s (3 min): Total runtime checked automatically. Opening & closing titles are included.
  • 3
    Age Category: Age automatically assigns Category I (10–13 yrs) or Category II (14–18 yrs).
  • 4
    Format & Size: MP4, MOV, or WEBM container under 300 MB recommended.

Answers Next to Form

Common questions while filling
Does my smartphone brand matter? ▾
No! Any phone or tablet (Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, Pixel, iPad, etc.) is 100% eligible. What matters to our jury is your artistic originality, framing, and story—not expensive gear.
Do title cards count towards 3 minutes? ▾
Yes. Total Running Time (TRT) includes titles and credits and must not exceed 180 seconds. We suggest keeping titles to 3–5 seconds so the story has full spotlight.
Can teachers or parents help? ▾
Adults may offer logistical support, loan gear, and ensure safety. However, the concept, camera work, acting, and editing must be executed by youth filmmakers.
When are results announced? ▾
You receive email confirmation immediately. Selected shortlist films are announced before the live gala on April 17, 2026, where winners are honored.
