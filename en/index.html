Ąžuolynas Film Festival

The «Ažuolynas» Film Festival is named after one of the sights of Kaunas, at the foot of which our studio is located.

Festival Topic: “An unusual view at ordinary things”

Guidelines

Terms and Conditions

  • 1 The film must be shot on a phone/tablet;
  • 2 The film must be strictly up to 3 minutes;
  • 3 From one participant no more than one work;

Since the creative vision of children and their self-expression are important to us, works with the dominant participation of adults are not accepted at the festival. Films made only by children will be given priority.

Competition

Two Age Categories & Awards

Category I
From 10 to 13 years old
For budding visual artists beginning their cinematic storytelling journey.
Category II
From 14 to 18 years old
For youth directors exploring bold perspectives and nuanced compositions.
