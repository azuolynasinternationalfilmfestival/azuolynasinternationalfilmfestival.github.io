Submission Guidelines FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 3-minute runtime rule, smartphone/tablet camera criteria, and adult guidance policies before submitting.

Device Requirements Which devices can be used for filming? Does the phone brand or model matter?
▾

Any smartphone or tablet is 100% eligible. You can shoot with an iPhone, Samsung, Xiaomi, Google Pixel, Huawei, iPad, or any Android/iOS device.

The model year does not matter—whether you have the newest flagship device or an older phone, what matters most to our jury is your artistic vision, framing, storytelling, and unique view on ordinary things.

Device Requirements Can I use phone accessories like tripods, gimbals, clip-on lenses, or external microphones?
▾

Yes, mobile accessories are permitted! You are encouraged to use:

  • Tripods and mounts for stable framing;
  • Mobile gimbals or handheld stabilizers for smooth tracking shots;
  • Clip-on lenses (wide-angle, macro, or anamorphic) attached to your phone camera;
  • External microphones (lavalier/lapel mics, shotgun mics, or wireless mic transmitters) plugged directly into your phone or tablet for clear dialogue.
Key criteria: The actual image recording must originate directly from the smartphone or tablet camera sensor.
Device Requirements Can I shoot with a DSLR, mirrorless camera, GoPro, or cinema camera?
▾

No. Standalone digital cameras (DSLRs, mirrorless cameras, camcorders, GoPros/action cams, or drones) are strictly prohibited.

Festival Rule 1 exists to create a truly equal, accessible playing field for young creators. We believe smartphones and tablets are universal democratic tools that challenge students to focus on visual storytelling rather than expensive studio gear.

Device Requirements Can I edit my film on a laptop or computer using desktop editing software?
▾

Yes! You are completely free to edit wherever you like.

While the raw video footage must be captured using your smartphone or tablet, the post-production stage (editing, titling, sound design, music mixing, and color grading) can be done either on mobile apps (such as CapCut, VN Video Editor, iMovie, LumaFusion) or on a computer (such as DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere, Final Cut Pro, or Clipchamp).

Device Requirements Can I submit animation or stop-motion films captured on a phone/tablet?
▾

Yes, absolutely! Stop-motion animation, claymation, and puppet animation shot frame-by-frame using a phone or tablet (for example with Stop Motion Studio or similar apps) are warmly welcomed in our festival categories.

3-Minute Limit Does the 3-minute limit include the opening title card and closing credits?
▾

Yes. The 3-minute limit (strictly ≤ 180 seconds) is Total Running Time (TRT).

It is measured from the very first video frame to the final black screen or credit card.

Pro-tip for filmmakers: Keep your opening title brief (3–5 seconds) and your closing credits concise (5–8 seconds) so that the remaining time is dedicated to your narrative.
3-Minute Limit What happens if my film is 3 minutes and 2 seconds (182 seconds)?
▾

Submissions that exceed 180 seconds cannot be accepted for the official competition.

Our submission form automatically inspects video duration when you select your file. If your video is longer than 180 seconds, the upload will be rejected with an alert. Please trim a few frames or speed up end credits in your editing timeline before submitting.

3-Minute Limit Is there a minimum duration? Can my film be 30 seconds or 1 minute?
▾

There is no minimum duration requirement!

Short-form filmmaking is a recognized art form. Micro-shorts (30s, 60s, or 90s) are just as eligible for awards as longer works, provided they tell a compelling story or offer an original visual viewpoint.

3-Minute Limit What video formats, resolutions, and file sizes are accepted?
▾

We accept the most standard video container formats:

  • Formats: MP4, MOV, or WEBM (encoded with H.264 or H.265 video codec and AAC audio);
  • Aspect Ratio: Both horizontal (16:9 widescreen) and vertical (9:16 mobile format) are accepted;
  • Resolution: 1080p Full HD (1920×1080) is recommended. 4K is also accepted;
  • File Size: Please keep video files under 300 MB for seamless web streaming and review.
Student Eligibility Who is eligible to participate in the festival?
▾

The festival is open to school students, cinema studio members, and independent young filmmakers worldwide aged 10 to 18 years old:

  • Category I: 10 to 13 years old;
  • Category II: 14 to 18 years old.
Mentors & Ethics How much can teachers, parents, or adult mentors help?
▾

Adult participation must strictly be limited to:

  • Providing safety and logistical supervision;
  • Lending equipment (phones, microphones, computers);
  • Helping with file exports or form submission.

The core creative process must be 100% student-driven: writing the concept, holding the camera, directing, editing, and acting must be done by children. Films exhibiting dominant adult execution will be disqualified.

Submission Limit Can I submit more than one film? Can multiple students co-direct?
▾

Each participant can submit no more than one work per festival edition (Rule 3).

Co-directed films by small student teams or school classes are welcomed; in such cases, please list the lead student director on the submission form and mention your co-creators in the institution or synopsis fields.

Ready to submit your short film?
